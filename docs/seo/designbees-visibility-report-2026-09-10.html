2 · Top three priorities Recommendation

Turn head-term impressions into clicks

"graphic design services" earned 2,106 impressions in a quarter on page 1 with 0% CTR; "graphic design services australia" 373 at 0.3%; "unlimited graphic design" 381 at position 31. Clicks are brand-led: in August "design bees" and "designbees" were 47 of the top 49 query clicks. Rewrite the title, meta description and first 100 words of the homepage and of /design-solutions/graphic-design as direct answers with the $545 entry price, then read CTR in Search Console after four weeks. The homepage and pricing guide already carry 68% of the site's Google AI-feature impressions, so a sharper answer on them is read by AI Overviews as well.

Why it matters: it is the largest volume on the site with the least return, and it needs no new pages. Semrush already flags the homepage title (61 characters) and meta description (149 characters) as over length.

Clear the technical flags the tools agree on

Fix the Product-snippet and Merchant-listing schema fields Google flagged on 9 Sep (missing review, aggregateRating, availability, return policy, shipping, identifier), the HTTP internal link and redirect chain from Ahrefs, the missing image alt text, and the Interaction to Next Paint (6.19 s) and Total Blocking Time (1.07 s) failures (audit which Wix apps and scripts load on every page; Wix Hotels and Wix Chat are installed).

Why it matters: schema on the pricing and package pages is what both rich results and AI engines read, and those are already the most-cited pages. INP failing hits the 29% of August clicks that were mobile.

Close the AI-visibility gap off-site

Semrush AI score 0 and HubSpot share of voice 0/10 on ChatGPT and Gemini say the assistants are not choosing Design Bees for category prompts, even though Perplexity and Google AI Overview named it first in June. Google's own AI features do show the site (7,865 impressions in three months), so the gap is specifically ChatGPT and Gemini. Publish the Design Pickle alternative page, claim Clutch, G2 and DesignRush profiles, and collect five client reviews there.

Why it matters: the June audit found ChatGPT recommending ManyPixels, Penji and Kimp for "best unlimited graphic design subscription in Australia". Answer engines cite review sites and comparison lists. This was the #1 gap on the 24 July checklist and is still open.
