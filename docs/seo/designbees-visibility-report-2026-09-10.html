2 · Top three priorities Recommendation

Turn head-term impressions into clicks

"graphic design services" earned 1,787 impressions in the last three months at position 4.6 with 0.22% CTR (it was position 10.6 in June, so the ranking work has landed and the snippet has not); "graphic design services sydney" sits at position 2.1 with 122 impressions and no clicks; "brand design" 1,277 impressions at position 17.5. Clicks are brand-led: 212 of the 329 clicks in the queries export are the brand name. Rewrite the title, meta description and first 100 words of the homepage and of /design-solutions/graphic-design as direct answers with the $545 entry price, then read CTR in Search Console after four weeks. The homepage and pricing guide already carry 68% of the site's Google AI-feature impressions, so a sharper answer on them is read by AI Overviews as well.

Why it matters: it is the largest volume on the site with the least return, and it needs no new pages. Semrush already flags the homepage title (61 characters) and meta description (149 characters) as over length.

Clear the technical flags the tools agree on

Fix the Product-snippet and Merchant-listing schema fields Google flagged on 9 Sep (missing review, aggregateRating, availability, return policy, shipping, identifier), the HTTP internal link and redirect chain from Ahrefs, the missing image alt text, and the Interaction to Next Paint (6.19 s) and Total Blocking Time (1.07 s) failures (audit which Wix apps and scripts load on every page; Wix Hotels and Wix Chat are installed).

Why it matters: schema on the pricing and package pages is what both rich results and AI engines read, and those are already the most-cited pages. INP failing hits the 29% of August clicks that were mobile.

Close the gap where the engines still don't pick you

Peekaboo puts Design Bees at 56, ahead of ManyPixels, Penji, Design Pickle and Kimp, so the brand is not invisible. The gap is specific: 0 mentions in 8 runs on the unbranded "freelancer got too costly" prompt, and 0 on both ChatGPT and Gemini for "graphic design services (Australia)" where Google AI Overview scores 100. The engines build those answers from other companies' comparison posts: manypixels.co (27 citations), reelunlimited.com.au (22), flocksy.com (21), digitalpolo.com (18), graphiker.com.au (18), reddit.com (16), sourceforge.net (11), g2.com (3). Publish the Design Pickle alternative and freelancer-replacement pages, get listed on the directories that accept submissions, and claim G2 and Clutch.

Why it matters: the June audit found ChatGPT recommending ManyPixels, Penji and Kimp for this exact category, and Peekaboo now shows the mechanism: ChatGPT reaches those names through those companies' own listicles. Two of the five prompts a buyer would actually type return nothing. This was the #1 gap on the 24 July checklist and is still open.

D5 · Reconciling Peekaboo, HubSpot and Semrush

Three tools produced three different pictures on the same day, and the difference is not error, it is scope:

  • Peekaboo, 56 and leading. Five prompts, four of them chosen by us, all naming Australia or a named competitor. This measures how Design Bees does on the questions it is positioned for, and the answer is well.
  • HubSpot, 43 to 51. Its own generated prompt set for "unlimited graphic design subscription" in Australia, weighting sentiment at 40 of 100. It agrees that sentiment is good and share of voice is thin, and it agrees on which engines are weak.
  • Semrush free checker, 0. A broad, unchosen prompt sample built from the domain. This measures how Design Bees does on the whole category conversation, and there the answer is barely at all: 2 mentions, and the two topics it surfaced for were home decor and custom t-shirts, neither of which is the business.

Both readings are true at once, and together they are more useful than either alone: Design Bees wins the Australian framing and loses the generic one. The freelancer prompt scoring 0 in Peekaboo is the bridge between the two, because it is the one prompt of the five that does not name Australia or a competitor, and it behaves like a Semrush prompt rather than a Peekaboo one.
