D5 · Reconciling Peekaboo, HubSpot and Semrush

Three tools produced three different pictures on the same day, and the difference is not error, it is scope:

  • Peekaboo, 56 and leading. Five prompts, four of them chosen by us, all naming Australia or a named competitor. This measures how Design Bees does on the questions it is positioned for, and the answer is well.
  • HubSpot, 43 to 51. Its own generated prompt set for "unlimited graphic design subscription" in Australia, weighting sentiment at 40 of 100. It agrees that sentiment is good and share of voice is thin, and it agrees on which engines are weak.
  • Semrush free checker, 0. A broad, unchosen prompt sample built from the domain. This measures how Design Bees does on the whole category conversation, and there the answer is barely at all: 2 mentions, and the two topics it surfaced for were home decor and custom t-shirts, neither of which is the business.

Both readings are true at once, and together they are more useful than either alone: Design Bees wins the Australian framing and loses the generic one. The freelancer prompt scoring 0 in Peekaboo is the bridge between the two, because it is the one prompt of the five that does not name Australia or a competitor, and it behaves like a Semrush prompt rather than a Peekaboo one.
