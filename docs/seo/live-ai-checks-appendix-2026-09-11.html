J0 · A correction, and what it means for reading this appendix

The first capture script was unreliable, and this appendix was wrong before it was corrected. It read each page after a fixed delay rather than waiting for the answer to finish generating, so a slow response was recorded as an empty one, and an empty one was scored as "brand not mentioned". Four readings were affected, every one of them a false negative: the cost and best-subscription queries on ChatGPT, and the cost and freelancer queries on Google AI Mode. All four actually do name Design Bees. An earlier draft of this appendix concluded from the first three that Peekaboo's ChatGPT score was "inverted" against the real product. That conclusion was wrong and has been removed. Nothing from that draft reached the report.

The fault only ever ran one way: a truncated read can invent an absence, but it cannot invent a brand mention. So every negative in the first pass has since been re-checked, and the positives were left as recorded. Three were re-tested by script, using a method that waits for ChatGPT's stop-generating control to disappear and then requires the answer text to hold steady across four consecutive polls, run twice per query. The fourth, the freelancer query on Google AI Mode, was run by hand (docs/seo/data/live-ai-checks-manual-2026-09-11.md). After that sweep one negative survives: ChatGPT on the freelancer query, absent in 2 of 2 verified runs.

What this appendix is and is not. One or two runs per query, on one day, from one account and one location, with personalisation and location both in play. It is not an average. Its value is the opposite of Peekaboo's: Peekaboo repeats prompts across models to give a comparable score over time, while this shows the actual words, prices and links a buyer receives.

One genuine discrepancy remains, and it is instructive. Peekaboo scores ChatGPT 0 on the head category term while the live product puts Design Bees at the top. The likely cause is the one flagged in Appendix D2: Peekaboo samples ChatGPT as gpt-4o-mini, which has neither the live retrieval nor the local map results the consumer product uses, and this is precisely the query the live answer resolves through a map pack. J3 · What ChatGPT cites when it does name Design Bees

Across four verified runs, ChatGPT cited designbees.com.au every time. Two pages did the work:

  • / the homepage, cited in all four runs
  • /post/are-unlimited-graphic-design-services-truly-limitless-what-you-need-to-know, also cited in all four

That second page is worth noting: it does not appear anywhere in Peekaboo's top-50 cited URLs (Appendix D4), yet it is half of ChatGPT's evidence base for both commercial queries. Alongside it ChatGPT cited graphiker.com.au/pricing, digitalpolo.com and kimp.io. The rivals it names alongside Design Bees are Graphiker, DigitalPolo, Kimp, Design Pickle and Flocksy, so the Australian competitor set identified in D4 is confirmed on the live product.

Peekaboo's 0 does not contradict the AI Mode win, because Peekaboo never sampled AI Mode: it logged 0 runs on that engine (Appendix D2). The two measurements cover different surfaces.

The most useful detail is which page won it. Google AI Mode built its answer largely from /post/cannot-afford-a-full-time-designer-here-are-your-options, citing it five times, more than any other source including Reddit. So the content that answers this question already exists and already works. The problem is reach, not absence: that page is invisible to ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overview.

Two cautions. Even in the win, Design Bees is framed as a "local regional option" after "global platforms like Penji, Design Pickle, and ManyPixels", despite being the source the answer is built on. And on ChatGPT the query names nobody: it describes the models generically and cites nothing, so that surface is uncontested rather than lost to a rival, which makes it cheaper to win than a term competitors already hold.

J5 · Two levers this check surfaced that the rest of the report missed

  • Google Business Profile drives the location answers. Both engines returned a map pack for the Australia and Sydney queries, with "Design Bees - Unlimited Graphic Design, 5.0, Surry Hills" as a map entity rather than a web result. That is the Business Profile, not the website, and it is the reason ChatGPT ranks Design Bees top on the head term where Peekaboo scores 0. Both engines also displayed it as "Closed" at the time of asking, so hours are being read and shown to buyers.
  • Clutch is cited by both engines and there is no claimed profile. AI Mode led its Australia answer with Clutch directory pages, and ChatGPT cited Clutch's Sydney page. The report already recommended claiming Clutch on general principle; this is direct evidence for it.
