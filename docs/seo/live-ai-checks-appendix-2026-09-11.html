J2 · Peekaboo's ChatGPT score does not match the real ChatGPT

On the three prompts measurable both ways, the two disagree, and not by a little:

  • Peekaboo scored the cost prompt 100 and best-subscription 88. Real ChatGPT names Design Bees in neither.
  • Peekaboo scored ChatGPT 0 on "graphic design services (Australia)". Real ChatGPT puts Design Bees top, with the correct price.

The cause is in Appendix D2: Peekaboo samples ChatGPT as gpt-4o-mini, a small model without the live retrieval and local results the consumer product uses. The practical rule: treat Peekaboo as a relative tracker across time, not as a description of what a customer sees. Where the two conflict, the live check is the better guide to buyer experience, and Peekaboo is the better guide to whether things are improving month on month.

J5 · Two levers this check surfaced that the rest of the report missed

  • Google Business Profile drives the location answers. Both ChatGPT and AI Mode returned a map pack for the Australia and Sydney queries, with "Design Bees - Unlimited Graphic Design, 5.0, Surry Hills" as a map entity rather than a web result. That is the Business Profile, not the website, and it is currently the strongest asset in the two queries where Design Bees performs best on ChatGPT. Both engines also showed it as "Closed" at the time of asking, so opening hours are being read and displayed.
  • Clutch is cited by both engines and there is no claimed profile. AI Mode led its Australia answer with Clutch directory pages, and ChatGPT cited Clutch's Sydney page. The report already recommended claiming Clutch on general principle; this is direct evidence for it.
