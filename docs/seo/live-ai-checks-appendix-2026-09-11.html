Six buyer queries run on 11 Sep 2026 (AEST) from AJ's own machine and Australian connection: ChatGPT in a signed-in browser session, and Google AI Mode via google.com/search?udm=50&hl=en-AU&gl=au. This is the check the cloud session could not perform, because Google served its "unusual traffic" challenge to the datacentre IP. Three results were re-tested twice each; see J0.

J0 · A correction, and what it means for reading this appendix

The first capture script was unreliable, and this appendix was wrong before it was corrected. It read each page after a fixed delay rather than waiting for the answer to finish generating, so a slow response was recorded as an empty one, and an empty one was scored as "brand not mentioned". Three readings were affected: the cost query on Google AI Mode, and the cost and best-subscription queries on ChatGPT. All three actually do name Design Bees. An earlier draft of this appendix concluded from those false negatives that Peekaboo's ChatGPT score was "inverted" against the real product. That conclusion was wrong and has been removed. It never reached the report.

The corrected method waits for ChatGPT's stop-generating control to disappear, then requires the answer text to stay unchanged across four consecutive polls before reading it, and runs each query twice. The re-tested figures below come from that method. The three results not re-tested were all positives, which the fault could not manufacture: a truncated read can invent an absence but it cannot invent a brand mention.

What this appendix is and is not. One or two runs per query, on one day, from one account and one location, with personalisation and location both in play. It is not an average. Its value is the opposite of Peekaboo's: Peekaboo repeats prompts across models to give a comparable score over time, while this shows the actual words, prices and links a buyer receives.

One genuine discrepancy remains, and it is instructive. Peekaboo scores ChatGPT 0 on the head category term while the live product puts Design Bees at the top. The likely cause is the one flagged in Appendix D2: Peekaboo samples ChatGPT as gpt-4o-mini, which has neither the live retrieval nor the local map results the consumer product uses, and this is precisely the query the live answer resolves through a map pack. So the caveat in D2 holds, but narrowly: Peekaboo under-reports answers that are driven by local and map results. It is not wrong across the board, and on three of four comparable prompts it matches the live product.

J3 · What ChatGPT cites when it does name Design Bees

Across four verified runs, ChatGPT cited designbees.com.au every time. Two pages did the work:

  • / the homepage, cited in all four runs
  • /post/are-unlimited-graphic-design-services-truly-limitless-what-you-need-to-know, also cited in all four

That second page is worth noting: it does not appear anywhere in Peekaboo's top-50 cited URLs (Appendix D4), yet it is half of ChatGPT's evidence base for both commercial queries. Alongside it ChatGPT cited graphiker.com.au/pricing, digitalpolo.com and kimp.io. The rivals it names alongside Design Bees are Graphiker, DigitalPolo, Kimp, Design Pickle and Flocksy, so the Australian competitor set identified in D4 is confirmed on the live product.

J4 · The one real gap, now firmly established

"What do small businesses typically switch to when their freelance designer becomes too costly" is the single failure common to every measurement taken: Peekaboo 0 across 8 runs, live ChatGPT absent across 2 verified runs, Google AI Mode absent. It is also the query most resistant to the capture fault, because it was re-tested with the corrected method and still returned nothing.

The shape of the failure matters. ChatGPT does not name a competitor instead: it names nobody, describing the options generically (subscription, cheaper freelancer, offshore, in-house junior, DIY with Canva) and citing nothing at all. Google AI Mode fills the space with Reddit threads. So this is not a query Design Bees is losing to a rival, it is one no provider currently owns. That makes it cheaper to win than a contested term, and it is the closest query of the six to how a buyer describes the problem before they know the category exists.

J5 · Two levers this check surfaced that the rest of the report missed

  • Google Business Profile drives the location answers. Both engines returned a map pack for the Australia and Sydney queries, with "Design Bees - Unlimited Graphic Design, 5.0, Surry Hills" as a map entity rather than a web result. That is the Business Profile, not the website, and it is the reason ChatGPT ranks Design Bees top on the head term where Peekaboo scores 0. Both engines also displayed it as "Closed" at the time of asking, so hours are being read and shown to buyers.
  • Clutch is cited by both engines and there is no claimed profile. AI Mode led its Australia answer with Clutch directory pages, and ChatGPT cited Clutch's Sydney page. The report already recommended claiming Clutch on general principle; this is direct evidence for it.
