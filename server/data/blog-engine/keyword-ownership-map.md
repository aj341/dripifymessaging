# Design Bees Blog: Writer Brief, Keyword Ownership Map

**Date:** 26 July 2026
**For:** anyone writing or pitching blog content for designbees.com.au
**Source:** live Search Console data (performance tracker, snapshot 24 Jul 2026), master content plan, and the winnable-content strategy (7 Jul 2026).

## Why this document exists

Earlier this year five of our ten posts were fighting over two keyword clusters, and Google ranked none of them. We spent June and July consolidating: three posts were 301-redirected into pillars, every surviving page was assigned exactly one keyword cluster, and rankings recovered. Every new post you write must slot into this system, not re-break it.

**The one rule: one keyword cluster, one page.** Before you pitch or write anything, check the target keyword against the ownership table below. If a page already owns it, you either don't write it, or you write something that supports the owner and links to it.

- ~~What are unlimited graphic design services~~ → folded into the unlimited explainer

From the winnable-content strategy, these have live demand and no owner on our site or in the AU SERP:

3. **"Is a design subscription worth it? (Australia)"** consideration/nurture piece.
4. **Long-tail intents with impressions and no dedicated page:** unlimited video design service, outsource design / design outsourcing australia, freelance designer vs subscription cost maths.
5. **2026 refresh of the trends post** (update in place, keep the URL).

## How to pitch anything else

State the exact target keyword, check it against the ownership table and the contested list above, and confirm no owner exists. If the topic is adjacent to an owned cluster, the post must target a clearly different intent and link to the owning page with an exact-match anchor. When in doubt, ask AJ before drafting, one Slack line saves a wasted article.
